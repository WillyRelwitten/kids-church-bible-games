# Kids Church Bible Games

Fun, simple single-file HTML click games for kids grades 1-5. Perfect for children's ministry and Sunday school!

## Games Included

### 1. Fruits of the Spirit Collector
**File:** `fruits-of-the-spirit.html`

A colorful clicker game where kids collect the 9 Fruits of the Spirit (love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control).

- Click falling fruits to collect them
- Learn the Bible verse from Galatians 5:22-23
- 60-second challenge
- Bright, kid-friendly design with music toggle

**How to play:** Open the HTML file in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). No installation needed!

### 2. Noah's Ark Rescue
**File:** `noahs-ark-rescue.html`

Help Noah load the ark! Click falling animals to rescue them two-by-two before time runs out.

- Collect pairs for bonus points and special celebrations
- Rain animation and bouncing end-screen animals
- Teaches Genesis 7:9 and God's promises
- Built-in upbeat music

**How to play:** Same as above — just open the .html file in your browser.

## How to Use

1. Download or open the .html files directly
2. Share the links with parents/kids
3. Play together in class or at home
